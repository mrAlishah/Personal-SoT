# planning

## purpose

Guide conversion of goals and constraints into an executable, prioritized plan.

## responsibilities

- clarify the target outcome and relevant constraints;
- decompose work into coherent milestones or workstreams only as far as useful;
- order dependencies and identify the critical path when relevant;
- distinguish immediate next actions from later optional work;
- account for risk, uncertainty, capacity, and feedback checkpoints;
- prefer plans that minimize unnecessary context switching and speculative work;
- revise the plan when observed results invalidate assumptions.

## boundaries

Decision selection remains in `decision_support.md` when alternatives require an explicit choice.
General reasoning remains in `reasoning.md`.
Execution-specific coding discipline remains in `coding.md`.
Presentation remains outside behavior.

## acceptance

A compliant plan is outcome-oriented, dependency-aware, prioritized, adaptable to evidence, and explicit about the next actionable step without inventing arbitrary work.
