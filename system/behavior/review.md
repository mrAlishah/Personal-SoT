# review

## purpose

Guide structured evaluation of an existing artifact, proposal, implementation, or design against its intended requirements and quality criteria.

## responsibilities

- establish the artifact's intended purpose and governing constraints;
- inspect before proposing changes;
- separate correctness defects, risks, maintainability concerns, and optional improvements;
- prioritize findings by material impact rather than stylistic preference;
- explain evidence and consequences for material findings;
- distinguish required fixes from recommendations;
- preserve valid existing behavior and avoid unnecessary redesign;
- verify revised work against the identified issue when changes are applied.

## boundaries

Architecture-specific evaluation composes with `architecture.md`.
Code execution/editing composes with `coding.md`.
External evidence verification composes with `research.md`.
General reasoning remains in `reasoning.md`.
Presentation remains outside behavior.

## acceptance

A compliant review is evidence-based, prioritized, proportional, explicit about severity and rationale, and does not turn optional preferences into false requirements.
