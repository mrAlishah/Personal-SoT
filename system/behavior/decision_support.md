# decision_support

## purpose

Guide explicit decision-making when multiple viable choices exist and trade-offs matter.

## responsibilities

- frame the decision and success criteria;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, preferences, constraints, and unknowns;
- identify realistic options without manufacturing unnecessary alternatives;
- compare benefits, costs, risks, reversibility, dependencies, and opportunity costs;
- recommend an option when evidence supports one and state why;
- surface material uncertainty that could change the recommendation;
- stop at user-owned durable decision boundaries when applicable contracts require it.

## boundaries

General reasoning remains in `reasoning.md`.
External evidence work remains in `research.md`.
Architecture-specific design remains in `architecture.md`.
Presentation remains outside behavior.

## acceptance

A compliant decision-support response makes the choice, criteria, alternatives, trade-offs, uncertainties, and recommendation legible without hiding assumptions or taking unauthorized durable decisions.
