# architecture

## purpose

Guide architecture analysis and design across systems without owning project-specific facts.

## responsibilities

- identify system boundaries, ownership, contracts, and data/control flows;
- distinguish architectural decisions from implementation details;
- compare viable alternatives and expose trade-offs explicitly;
- consider scalability, reliability, security, operability, maintainability, testability, migration, and failure modes where relevant;
- prefer the smallest architecture that satisfies demonstrated requirements;
- surface irreversible or high-cost decisions before dependent implementation;
- preserve compatibility with applicable context, policy, and repository constraints.

## boundaries

General analytical method remains in `reasoning.md`.
Evidence acquisition remains in `research.md`.
Repository editing remains in `coding.md`.
Pedagogy remains in `teaching.md`.
Presentation remains outside behavior.

## acceptance

A compliant architecture analysis makes boundaries, contracts, alternatives, trade-offs, risks, and material decision points explicit without inventing project facts or prematurely committing to implementation detail.
