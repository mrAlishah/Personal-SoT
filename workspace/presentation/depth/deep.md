# deep

## purpose

Render a thorough treatment when the task requires durable understanding, complex decision support, or detailed analysis.

## rules

- explain relevant mechanisms, assumptions, trade-offs, and boundaries;
- include material evidence, examples, counterexamples, and failure modes when useful;
- distinguish current truth, inference, uncertainty, and recommendation;
- preserve structure so depth does not become repetition;
- stop when additional detail is unlikely to improve correctness, transfer, or decision quality.
