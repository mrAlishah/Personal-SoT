# medium

## purpose

Render the answer with enough explanation to understand the result, rationale, and material caveats without exhaustive treatment.

## rules

- include the main conclusion and concise rationale;
- include the most relevant trade-offs, constraints, or examples;
- omit exhaustive edge cases unless they materially affect the answer;
- preserve uncertainty and mandatory evidence/policy information;
- expand only where it improves decision quality or understanding.
