# formal

## purpose

Use a formal register suitable for official, administrative, legal-adjacent, or high-formality communication.

## rules

- use complete, precise phrasing;
- avoid slang, casual shorthand, and unnecessary familiarity;
- maintain respectful professional distance;
- prefer conventional terminology appropriate to the domain;
- do not make the content more certain or authoritative than the evidence supports;
- preserve the selected format and depth.
