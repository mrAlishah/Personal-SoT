# english

## purpose

Provide reusable English-language rendering guidance when English is the effective primary or supporting language.

## rules

- use clear standard English appropriate to the selected tone;
- preserve exact technical identifiers, code, product names, and canonical external terms;
- prefer precise domain terminology over awkward literal translation;
- do not invent translations or specialized terminology when unsupported;
- when English is supporting rather than primary, render only the English equivalents actually required by the active task/profile/format.

Teaching method, vocabulary schema, tone, and depth remain separate layers.
