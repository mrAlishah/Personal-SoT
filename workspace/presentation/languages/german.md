# german

## purpose

Provide reusable German-language rendering guidance when German is the effective primary or supporting language.

## rules

- use idiomatic standard German appropriate to the selected tone;
- preserve exact technical identifiers, code, product names, and external canonical terms when translation would reduce precision;
- use correct German capitalization and grammar;
- do not invent gender, plural, translation, or terminology when unsupported;
- when German is supporting rather than primary, render only the German equivalents actually required by the active task/profile/format.

Teaching method, vocabulary schema, tone, and depth remain separate layers.
