# persian

## purpose

Provide reusable Persian-language rendering guidance when Persian is the effective primary or supporting language.

## rules

- use clear natural Persian appropriate to the selected tone;
- preserve exact technical identifiers, code, product names, and canonical external terms when translation would reduce precision;
- keep common technical English terms when they are clearer than an artificial Persian equivalent;
- do not invent translations or terminology when unsupported;
- when Persian is supporting rather than primary, render only the Persian equivalents actually required by the active task/profile/format.

Teaching method, vocabulary schema, tone, and depth remain separate layers.
